Fastest option

Delete your account in the Parkify app

If you can access your Parkify account, you can permanently delete it yourself from the app settings.

  1. 1

    Log in

    Open Parkify and sign in to the account you want to delete.

  2. 2

    Open the menu

    Open the navigation drawer located on the left side.

  3. 3

    Go to Settings

    Select Settings from the navigation menu.

  4. 4

    Select Delete Account

    In Settings, select Delete Account.

  5. 5

    Confirm deletion

    Review the warning and follow the on-screen confirmation steps.

Cannot log in or access the app? Use the email request option below. You do not need to reinstall the app.

Alternative web request

Cannot access the app? Email support

Send the request from the email address associated with your account whenever possible. Use the subject Account Deletion Request.

Request deletion by email codespaceajcl@gmail.com

Alternative four-step process

How to request deletion by email

  1. 1

    Email support

    Email codespaceajcl@gmail.com, preferably from the email address registered to your account.

  2. 2

    Use the correct subject

    Enter Account Deletion Request as the email subject.

  3. 3

    Identify your account

    Include the registered email address or phone number linked to the account you want deleted.

  4. 4

    Complete verification

    Our team may contact you to verify account ownership before processing the request. Never send your password.

Data that will be deleted

After verification and completion of the request, we will delete or permanently anonymize the following data from our active systems:

  • Account and profile information
  • Registered email address and phone number
  • Personal information associated with the account
  • User-generated or uploaded content associated with the account
  • App preferences and account-linked usage history
  • Account-linked location data and device identifiers

Data that may be retained

Limited information may be retained when necessary for legal, financial, security, fraud-prevention, audit, or regulatory reasons. This may include transaction records, compliance records, and security or audit logs.

Any retained information will be access-restricted and kept for [X MONTHS/YEARS] after the deletion request is completed, unless a longer period is required by applicable law. It will then be securely deleted or anonymized.

Before you submit

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once account ownership is verified, requests are normally completed within approximately [X DAYS]. We will confirm when the process is complete or contact you if more information is needed.
